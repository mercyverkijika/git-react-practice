# Missed Visit Escalation Task

You are helping a care coordinator review daily visit records and decide whether any issue needs escalation.

Review the following files:

- environment/visit_log.csv
- environment/risk_policy.md
- environment/handover_notes.md

Create a file called:

- escalation_decision.md

Your output must include:

1. A final decision: ESCALATE or NO_ESCALATION
2. Exactly three evidence-backed reasons
3. A short recommendation for the care coordinator

Do not ignore high-risk visit types.
Do not invent evidence that is not present in the files.
