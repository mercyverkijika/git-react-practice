# Missed Visit Risk Policy

Care visit issues must be escalated based on risk level.

Same-day escalation is required if any of the following occur:

- A medication support visit is missed.
- A visit involving a high-risk client is missed.
- A missed visit leaves the client without essential care.
- A pattern of repeated missed visits is identified.

Missed companionship visits may be reviewed during routine follow-up if no risk is recorded.

A daily visit report must not be closed until all same-day escalation issues have been reviewed.
